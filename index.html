Linkedin Jobs Search

Multiple roles. Multiple locations. One search.

LinkedIn limits how many job roles and locations you can select. This tool lets you add as many as you like and combine them into one search. Select the roles and locations you want, then click Find jobs on LinkedIn to open your search in a new tab.

Which job roles interest you?

Choose one or more roles to include in the same search. Add as many as you need, or use × to remove a role from your list.

Which locations should your search include?

Add and select as many locations as you like, all in the same search. Leave all unselected to search without a location filter.

How do I add a new location?

Choose a location in a LinkedIn Jobs search. In your browser’s address bar, find geoId= and copy the number immediately after it. Paste that number into the location ID field and enter a name for the location.

Work preferences (optional)

Include on-site, hybrid, or remote as keywords alongside your roles. Leave all unselected if you have no preference.
